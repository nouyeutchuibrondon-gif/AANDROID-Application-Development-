AGRIBOT – Intelligent Monitoring & Control System-

AGRIBOT is an advanced AI‑powered monitoring, analytics, and control platform designed to operate and supervise an autonomous pest‑control robot for agricultural fields, gardens, and home environments. The system combines computer vision, robotics, and environmental sensing to deliver a fully automated, chemical‑free pest‑management solution.

Vision Architecture
AGRIBOT uses a three‑camera vision system to enable high‑precision pest detection and safe robot navigation:

Dual side cameras capture and analyze insects in real time using embedded AI models for detection, classification, and pest‑density estimation.
Front‑facing navigation camera handles autonomous pathfinding, obstacle avoidance, and movement planning to ensure efficient and safe operation.
This multi‑camera setup allows the robot to gather rich environmental insights while maintaining autonomy in diverse terrains.

Environmental Sensing & Monitoring
The platform integrates multiple sensors to track conditions that influence pest activity. AGRIBOT monitors:

Temperature
Humidity
Soil moisture
Light intensity
These live environmental readings are visualized through interactive dashboards, helping users understand how changing conditions impact pest behavior and potential infestations.

AI Analytics & Insights
AGRIBOT goes beyond simple monitoring by providing predictive and analytical intelligence, including:

Pest density mapping across farms or home areas
Hotspot and infestation zone detection
Correlation analysis between environment and pest activity
Predictive models to anticipate pest outbreaks and high‑risk periods
These insights enable proactive decision‑making and optimized pest‑control strategies.

User Interface & Control
The system is accessible through both mobile and web platforms. Users can:

View live camera feeds from all three cameras
Track real‑time robot location and movement paths
Manage environmental sensor dashboards
Access historical performance logs, analytics reports, and robot activity records
Configure robot settings, schedules, or patrol routes
A Smarter Approach to Pest Control
AGRIBOT transforms traditional pest management by replacing reactive, chemical‑heavy methods with a data‑driven, autonomous, and environmentally friendly solution. It provides continuous protection, reduces human effort, and supports healthier ecosystems for agriculture and home environments.
